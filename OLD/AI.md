# AI Class

## Table of Contents

* Setup
  * Phone profile: Ensure your device has a single, intentional identity (name and image).
  * Google account: Create or align your primary Google account to match your phone's identity for seamless sign-in.
* LLM chatbots
  * Setup: Access core AI assistants through your browser and sign up using your Google account.
  * Chat: Conduct a simple, iterative text conversation to refine an idea or complete a small task.
  * Vision (Analyze Images & Video): Upload visual media and ask the AI to describe, analyze, or extract information from it.
  * Hearing (Analyze Audio & Video): Upload audio files or video and ask the AI to transcribe, summarize, or analyze the sound.
  * Project Memory (Logs & Readme): Implement a two-document system (log.md and README.md) to manage complex, multi-session projects.
* Image Generators
  * Setup: Create an account on a service like Midjourney or Leonardo.ai via your phone's browser.
  * Generate Image: Craft a detailed text prompt and generate your first custom image.
* Audio
  * Text to Speech: Convert written text into a spoken audio file using an AI voice.
  * Voice Cloning: Create a digital replica of a voice from a short audio sample.
  * Sound Effects: Generate specific sound effects (e.g., a door creak, laser blast) from text descriptions.
* Suno
  * Setup: Create an account on Suno.ai via your phone's browser.
  * Making a Track: Generate a complete musical track (instrumentals and vocals) from a text description.
