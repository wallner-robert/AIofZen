## What?

A transformation from yelling at screens to fluid collaboration. This guide captures the shift from treating AI as faulty tools to working with digital partners. It's about replacing frustration with flow, turning limitations into demonstrations of skill, and finding that sweet spot where human and machine intelligence click. The methodology emerged from hitting walls and discovering ways through them—not by forcing, but by adapting.
