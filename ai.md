## Access Tiers Explained

**Freemium Model**  
Permanently free core services

**Daily Credit Allowance**  
Fixed credits reset every 24 hours

**Monthly Credit Reset**  
Larger credit pools refresh each month

**Subscription Model**  
Monthly or annual payments for unlimited access  
*(Yearly subscriptions typically 30-50% cheaper than monthly)*

## Casting Notes

*Multi-modal output often requires specific app features or may be limited*  
*API access is commercial pay-per-use, even for freemium models*  
*Regional restrictions may affect subscription availability*  
*Web access is the standard - apps are bonuses*

**The Zen Principle:** Each collaborator has its moment. The art is knowing which to cast for each scene.
